AMC & CMC - Annual Maintenance Contract


An Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) is a service agreement where a company or individual pays a provider for regular maintenance and repair services for a set period, typically one year. This contract ensures equipment or property is kept in optimal working condition, minimizes downtime, and provides predictable maintenance costs. AMCs are common for various assets, including machinery, vehicles, and computer systems.


  Routine scheduled maintenance of systems

  Non-comprehensive - spare parts not included, only labour and service

  OEMs or certified service providers

  CMC - Comprehensive Maintenance Contract

  long-term government contracts or PPP (public-private partnership) models i.e. GOCO

  obsolescence management and life-extension services
